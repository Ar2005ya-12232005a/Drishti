DRISHTI

दृष्टि, meaning sight

Every follow-up, in sight.

DRISHTI gives care teams one clear view of which follow-up appointments were missed, how long they have been waiting, and who is responsible for them.

Nothing slips through the gap

Follow-ups get lost between busy shifts and full inboxes. DRISHTI keeps them in one place so the team can see them at a glance.

See what slipped

Missed appointments are listed with the number of days they are overdue, longest wait first.

Know who owns it

Each follow-up is assigned to a named care-team member, so no case is left unclaimed.

Share it as a page

Any patient's status can be opened in a browser or saved as a page to send along.
